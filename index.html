A slow start to every day

Amber & Oak opened in 2026 above a wood shop, and the smell of sawdust never quite left the beams. We roast in small batches twice a week, pull every shot to order, and keep the tables mismatched on purpose. Some of our regulars have been coming since the first morning we unlocked the door.
